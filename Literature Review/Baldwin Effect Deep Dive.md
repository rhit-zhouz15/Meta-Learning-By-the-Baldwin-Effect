What is it?
- The Baldwin Effect is the costs and benefits of lifetime learning by individuals in an evolving population [2]
- Synergetic effect of evolving a population of learning individuals [2]
- Theory is that a learning population can accelerate evolution in some situations [2]
- Learning comes in many forms and is expensive [2]
- Learning is neural networks while instinct evolution is evolutionary algorithms (neural networks use more feedback from environment but evolution might eventually find a better way) [2]

Types of plasticity and learning
Phenotypic Plasticity - ability of organism to adapt to its environment (mainly physically)
- Smooths the fitness landscape by allowing individuals to explore the nearby phenotype (physical) space and sometimes both the phenotype and genotype space, making it easier for evolution to peak [2]
Phenotypic Rigidity - I think instinctive traits that come about through evolution and learning
- Can be helpful to augment or replace plastic mechanisms [2]
- Learning can be dangerous like through trial-and-error so having an instinct for certain things like avoiding a predator is more helpful [2]
Plasticity of behavior
- Behaviors are more plastic than physical traits because it relies on tuning the nervous system which is flexible and adaptable compared to other systems in the body [2]
- Learning behaviors also gives us more options to adapt to something and can be faster than waiting for physical evolution [2]
Plasticity of learning
- Learned behaviors are more flexible than instinctive ones and allows us to adapt to a wider range of environments [2]

Misconceptions
- Researchers have been mostly focusing solely on the benefits of lifetime learning but not the costs [2]
- Learning is not always good. It can speed up evolution in certain circumstances but slow it down in others [2]
- Evolution does not always choose for better learning, it constantly chooses between learning and instinct to find the best balance [2]

Baldwin effect vs Lamarckian inheritance
- Both say behaviors that are learned in one generation are kept in the next instinctively [2]
- Baldwin effect differs by saying that the ability to learn carries over but not the actual learned traits [2]
- Lamarckian says that the learned traits are also carried over [2]
- Lamarckian would say that a learned behavior would affect the DNA of the parent and give that gene to the child [2]
- Baldwin effect would say evolution will select for the ability to learn that behavior but not directly pass on the learned behavior itself [2]
- Lamarckian is not biologically accurate? or maybe even possible [2]
- Lamarckian ideal for evolutionary computation [2]

Learning vs instinct
Pros
- Learning better than instinct when new behavior is starting to evolve to allow a population to survive until an instinctive behavior can replace it [2]
- Learning better in dynamic environment since evolution can't adapt fast enough [2]
- Learning increases variation in population giving them better chance to survive [2]
- Learning is better for intermediate goals of an individual [2]
Cons
- More expensive than instinct, longer to learn like locally for some in the population and takes more energy (instinct is automatic for most in the population) [2]
- Doesn't spread to a population in a large scale as quickly [2]
- Learning requires the right kind of experience, not always available so it is less reliable [2]
- Vulnerable while learning that trait [2]
- Evolution is better for maximizing fitness of a population [2]
