### Evolution
- Across many generations of individuals
- Genome is changed (mostly initial weights or hyperparameters)
- There is a scalar fitness value for each individual
- Genome kept across generations (depending on Darwin, Baldwin, or Lamarckian)
### Learning
- Within the lifetime of one individual
- Weights are changed in that lifetime
- Has error and calculates gradient per-example loss
- Nothing is persisted unless Lamarckian
